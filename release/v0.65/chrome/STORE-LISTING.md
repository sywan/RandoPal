**繁體中文**

> 座位冒險現在可以直接輸入橫向列數與縱向行數，快速建立座位圖。畫面標出講台與前方；點選座位即可停用或恢復，再將名單隨機安排入座。原本的 CSV／Excel 座位表匯入方式仍可使用。

**English**

> Seat Shuffle now builds a seat map from the number of columns across and rows deep. A stage marker shows the front of the room. Tap seats to block or reopen them, then randomly assign people. CSV and Excel seat map imports remain available.
